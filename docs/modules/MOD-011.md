## Backend Capabilities & Data Models
- **Data Models**:
  - `TransformationRule`: Stores individual business rules (string manipulation, data cleaning, lookups, math expressions etc.) targeted at particular input/output fields.
- **Services & Controllers**:
  - `TransformationRuleService`: Handles CRUD and categorization/statistics for rules ensuring tenant isolation (`organizationId`).
  - `TransformationRuleController`: Validates API bindings for routes.
- **Routes**:
  - Mounted under `/api/organizations/:orgId/transformation-rules` and `/api/transformation-rules`.
